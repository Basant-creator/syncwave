Clock offset: master clock − device clock, ± half the best round trip (the error bound). Drift: clock = rate difference between the two clocks in ppm (needs ~20 s of samples); playback = what the device is playing minus where the master clock says it should be (ms, + = ahead). Output latency is what the browser reports; Bluetooth latency is usually not included.
